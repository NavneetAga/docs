---
title: Generate OTP
description: Send an OTP to a member's registered email or SMS channel using their email address.
openapi: "POST /lbms-ingress/member/api/Member/GenerateOTP"
---

## When to Use

Use this when the member's **email address** is known (i.e. `UniquerefID` is an email). The OTP is sent to the member's registered channel based on your program configuration.

If you know the member's `RelationReference` (CID) instead, use [Generate OTP by Relation Reference](/api-reference/otp/generate-otp-by-relation).

After a successful response, verify the OTP with [Verify OTP](/api-reference/otp/verify-otp).
