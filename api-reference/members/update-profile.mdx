---
title: Update Profile
description: Update an existing member's profile attributes.
openapi: "POST /lbms-ingress/member/api/Member/UpdateProfileWithAttributes"
---

<Note>
  At least one `data.*` attribute must be present in the payload. Custom attributes defined in your CPD schema can also be included under `data`.
</Note>

Obtain the `id` from the `Id` field returned by [Get Member Profile](/api-reference/members/get-member-profile).
